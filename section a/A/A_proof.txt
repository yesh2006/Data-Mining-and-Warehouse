SECTION A — A: SIMILARITY PROOF

Corpus facts:
- 12,000 notices
- 260 portals
- 900 trusted labelled pairs: 279 SAME, 621 DIFFERENT

Required labelled examples:
- SAME: N010018 vs N010020
- DIFFERENT: N007876 vs N008565

Measured aggregate similarity:
- Word-3: DIFFERENT mean 0.253793; SAME mean 0.644673; separation 0.3909
- Character-5: DIFFERENT mean 0.430489; SAME mean 0.708862; separation 0.2784

Decision:
Word-3 shingles are adopted for the main similarity representation because the measured
SAME-vs-DIFFERENT mean separation is larger in the 900-pair experiment.

Signal/noise evidence from the corpus:
- The SAME example is a corrigendum with different portal/reference/date values but the same work,
  showing that identifiers and dates should not dominate text similarity.
- The DIFFERENT example contains generic procurement boilerplate on a nodal portal while describing
  a different opportunity, showing that repeated boilerplate is not useful discriminative signal.
