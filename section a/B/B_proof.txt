SECTION A — B: MINHASH PROOF

Accuracy requirement used for engineering selection:
MAE <= 0.03 on the trusted labelled_pairs.csv experiment.

Measured:
- 32 permutations: MAE 0.0504
- 64 permutations: MAE 0.0336
- 128 permutations: MAE 0.0285

Selection:
128 permutations is the smallest tested signature size whose measured MAE is below 0.03.
The choice is therefore tied to an explicit accuracy requirement rather than a round-number convention.

The 128-permutation configuration is then used by the LSH stage.
